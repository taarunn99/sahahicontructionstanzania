YHSwellbar
Swellable Joint Sealing Waterbar
YUHONG
Technical Data Sheet (TDS)
DESCRIPTION
YHSwellbar is a hydrophilic rubber sealing strip that swells when in contact with water.
WHERE TO USE
• Construction joints in water retaining or excluding structures such as water tanks, swimming pools, pumping chambers, reservoirs, tunnels, basement, culverts, cable ducts etc.
• New-old concrete joints.
• Pipe and steel work penetrations through concrete floors and walls.
• Joints between floor slabs and pile caps.
CHARACTERISTICS AND PACKAGING
Item | Description
Packaging | 10 m × 10 rolls per box
Color | Red
Width / mm | 10-50
Thickness / mm | 10-50
ADVANTAGES
• Easy to install by adhesive bonding or mechanical fixation.
• Solves detailing problems in conjunction with other kind of waterstops.
• Swelling properties unaffected by long term wet/dry cycling.
• Tolerant of salts in concrete a n d groundwater.
• Sustains effective seal in wet conditions.
• Long-term reliability tested.
• Highly adaptable to accommodate complex join shapes.
• Provides effective combination of waterstops maintaining network continuity.
• No welding required.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Application
(1) Surface preparation
Long term durability and function can only be achieved with good preparation to ensure that water cannot by pass the water-stop.
It is recommended that concrete substrates have a minimum compressive strength of 20 N//mm², and at least 75 mm of concrete cover in all directions of the hydrophilic water-stop.
Ensure the concrete surfaces where the water-stop will be places are smooth, clean and free from contamination such as dust, oil, grease, organic growth & release agents.
For fresh concrete we recommended forming a groove in the middle of the joint using a suitable timber strip. After the concrete has hardened, remove the strip to reveal the groove in which the hydrophilic waterstop can sit with minimal preparation.
(2) Application
Maneuver the YHSwellbar to its final position within a few minutes of bonding and remove excess material before it dries. Use nail or shot fire the hydrophilic waterstop to the concrete substrate.
At the corners and intersections, simply butt join the waterstop together ensuring tight joint between the profiles. No welding is required for hydrophilic waterstops.
TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
The material is packed in plastic buckets and valve bags. During transportation, avoid exposure to sunlight, rain, and cold. Prevent extrusion and collision, and keep the packaging intact. YHSwellbar shall be stored in its sealed container under dry and shaded place in a temperature from 10 °C -35 °C .
PRECAUTIONS
• It is essential that both the hydrophilic waterstop & adhesive are kept dry prior to pouring concrete, otherwise expansion of the waterstop will take place.
• Concrete surfaces must be clean and free of all contaminants. Remove all concrete spatter and nibs.
• Do not use it for movement/expansion joints.
• In a totally dry state YHSwellbar shrinks to its original dimension, but will expand again when coming in contact with water.
• If it is to be fixed around small diameter pipes use additional mechanical fixing with tie wire or a sleeve.
