Water-based Bitumen Primer

Technical Data Sheet (TDS) 1 / 3
DESCRIPTION
YHBituprimer-4050 Water-based Bitumen Primer is is prepared with high-concentration polymer modified
emulsified asphalt as base material and high-performance emulsion. It is mainly used for Primer of
waterproof membrane before construction, so as to improve the adhesion between membrane and base, and
has the function of blocking water vapor penetration.
WHERE TO USE
YHBituprimer-4050 is used as a primer and sealer coat prior to the application of bituminous waterproofing
membranes and coatings.

ADVANTAGES
• Compatible with various materials, quick-drying, and high penetration power.
• The formula of super concentrated mother liquor can be diluted with water as required (not more than
1:2).
• High adhesion to the substrate.
• Improves the adhesion of bituminous membranes and coatings.
• Cold applied, does not require heating.
• Water-based product, environmentally friendly, and pollution-free.
• Anti corrosive.
• Conforms to ASTM D41 - Asphalt primer for roofing, damp proofing and waterproofing.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Reference dosage
The theoretical dosage is (0.1-0.15)kg/m2. If diluted, the dosage can be increased as appropriate.
Construction method
Spraying application is recommended
• Substrate preparation: The concrete substrate should be clean, dry, sound, and free of all contaminants
such as dirt, oil, and grease. Surface defects, such as blowholes and voids, must be repaired. Filling of joints
and surface leveling must be carried out before coating.
• Priming: It is recommended water-based bitumen primer toenhance the substrate preparation. Before
applying primer, thoroughly shake the container to ensure all solids are distributed throughout the
dispersion and a uniform consistency is achieved. Apply the primer by brush, roller or sprayer. Typically one
single application is required; porous substrate may require two or more coats of primer to effectively seal
the surface. To ensure proper adhesion, the membrane must be applied within 24 hours of the application of
primer, but only once the primer is dry to the touch. Lower temperatures and/or humid conditions may
extend the drying time.
TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
• Protect the product from direct sunlight, rain, and frost during transportation. Avoid squeezing or impact.
• Store in a dry,well-ventilated area, away from fire and heat sources.
• Recommended storage temperature:between 5℃and 35℃.
• Under standard storage and transportation conditions, the product has a shelf life of 9 months from the
date of production.
PRECAUTIONS
Read the product label and Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) before using it.Users should acquaint
themselves with all risks & safety regulations and conform to manufacturer's recommendations,
material handling and storage specifications.
• Users must familiarize themselves with all risks, safety regulations, and adhere to the manufacturer's
recommendations, including material handling and storage specifications.
• Once opened, the product should be used on the same day. Any remaining material must be properly
sealed and used as soon as possible.
• The recommended construction temperature range is 5°C to 35°C. Construction is prohibited during
rain, snow, or if such weather is predicted within 24 hours.
• Don't add too much water to avoid affecting product performance.
• Ensure the waterproof layer is applied evenly. Avoid walking on the surface before it is fully dried to
prevent damage to the waterproof layer.
