PMH-6010
HDPE Semi Transparent Pre-applied Waterproof Membrane
Technical Data Sheet (TDS) 1 / 4

DESCRIPTION
PMH-6010 HDPE Semi Transparent Pre-applied Membrane composites a HDPE film, pressure sensitive adhesive, protective coating and release liner. For 1.2m width membrane, one long edge has a reserved 80mm wide self-adhesive overlap as cold apply. For 2.4m width membrane, both long edge have 150mm wide welding strip, used for tunnel with large deformation or rough surface lining.

WHERE TO USE
PMH-6010 is designed for dim light, wet, dusty and vibration construction condition, suitable for various tunnels and underground space waterproofing projects, such as highway, railway, power, hydraulic, subway section tunnels and caverns ect.

ADVANTAGES
Fully adhered to post-cast concrete, no water migration, significantly enhances the reliability of the waterproofing system.
Lightweight and transparent material, the overlapping areas are more suitable for welding construction, convenient and efficient.
Excellent durability, resistant to chemical corrosion, can achieve the same design life as the structure.
Environmentally friendly, free from harmful chemicals, no pollution to water and soil.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Accessories: PMH-T-D80 Double-sided tape, PMH-T-C120 Smooth Cover Tape, PMH-M-67 Wire mesh gasket, PMH-S-600 Sealant, PMH-T-B250D Double-sided Butyl Tape, etc.
Construction Tools: Pressing plates, Rollers, Double-seam climbing Welder, Portable electromagnetic welder etc.
Application Instructions
Tunnel Arch Construction:
Initial Surface Treatment of Primary Support → Laying and Fixing Geotextile → Lay Pre-applied Waterproof Membranes → Overlap Treatment → Detailing at Nodes → Reinforcing Steel Bar Binding → Concrete Pouring.
Construction Key Point:
When the smoothness of the supporting surface does not meet the specification requirements (D/L ≥ 1/6), the technical personnel will decide on measures such as chiseling and leveling based on the actual conditions of the project.

When laying waterproofing membranes, sufficient loose laying allowance should be left based on the smoothness of the substrate.
The overlapping parts of flat and vertical membranes should be protected in advance to prevent contamination during concrete pouring, which could affect subsequent overlapping.

TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
Different types and specifications of products should be stored separately to avoid mixing.
When storing rolled materials horizontally, the height should not exceed five layers. When storing them vertically, they should be placed in a single layer. During transportation, prevent tilting or lateral pressure.
Avoid exposure to sunlight and rain. If necessary, cover with felt cloth. Ensure proper ventilation, and the storage temperature should not exceed 45°C.
Do not allow contact with acids, alkalis, oils, and organic solvents.
Under normal transportation and storage conditions, the storage period is one year from the production date.

PRECAUTIONS
Contractors must undergo training before starting work.
During construction, if it rains or snows, appropriate protective measures should be taken for the already laid materials.
It is strictly forbidden for personnel wearing studded shoes to enter the construction site. Special attention must be paid to site protection during the construction process, and smoking is strictly prohibited on the construction site.
The laying of materials is strictly prohibited during rainy or snowy weather, or in winds of level 5 or higher. The environment temperature for cold application of self-adhesive membranes and tapes should not be lower than 5°C (this includes storage, construction, and the 24 hours following the completion of construction).
If the overlapping edges lose their adhesion or are not properly sealed, they should be covered and pressed with tape, or the overlapping edges should be heated to enhance adhesion and pressed to form a whole.
When overlapping two lengths of self-adhesive membranes in the longitudinal direction, ensure the bonding surfaces are clean. After overlapping, the edges should be rolled and pressed firmly to ensure good adhesion.
The waterproof layer should not be damaged when pouring structural concrete, which should be completed within 15 days after the installation of the membranes.
