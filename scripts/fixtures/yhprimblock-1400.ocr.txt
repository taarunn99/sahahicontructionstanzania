YHPrimBlock-1400
Three-Component Epoxy-Cementitious Moisture Barrier
Technical Date Sheet (TDS) 1/3

DESCRIPTION
YHPrimBlock-1400 is a three-component epoxy-cementitious system which has the capacity of sealing on
damp surface to prevent blistering of resin based coatings, even if they are very smooth (ceramic tiles,
porcelain, marble, etc.), and of forming a compact layer suitable for laying other coverings and coatings. The
product may be used as it is, by diluting it accordingly with water, or by adding quartz sand, to obtain a
smoothing mortar for use on uneven concrete surfaces.

WHERE TO USE
It is suitable for use as a waterproofing primer coat for damp substrate, and especially:
(1) As a moisture barrier: it is suitable for substrate of high moisture substrates, even green concrete, and
allowing for the application of epoxy, polyurethane and other resin coatings requiring dry substrates.
(2) Extended with quartz sand, as a patching and repair mortar for smoothing over uneven surfaces. It is
recommended to add quartz sand (0.25mm-0.7mm) at a ratio of 1 : 0.2-0.5.

ADVANTAGES
• Easy application.
• Excellent bond to green or hardened concrete whether damp or dry.
• Non-solvent formula, suitable for internal use.
• Good dimensional stability similar to concrete.
• Impervious to liquid and water vapor.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
• Substrate preparation: The substrate must be clean, sound and free of rust, paint, grease, oil, laitance and
standing water. If the surface to be treated is uneven, it must be repaired and levelled off prior to treatment
with a suitable damp-resistant product, or with YHPrimBlock-1400 mixed with 0.25 Quartz or 0.5 Quartz
sand.
• Preparation of product: YHPrimBlock-1400 is made up of three components, of which two are liquids and
one is a powder. To prepare the product, mix component A with component B until a homogeneous,
uniform-coloured, mix is obtained. Then slowly add component C (powder) while mixing until all contents
are mixed in. The mixing ratios are strictly adhered to in order to avoid poor reaction of the product. The
mixing ratio between the three components is 1: 1: 4 in weight. It is recommend to use a low-speed drill to
avoid overheating the mix which may reduce the workability times and provoke air entrapment. After
mixing the three components together, and according to the application mehtod used, add water as
follows: (1) By brush: 5-10%; (2) By roller: 10-15%; (3) By spraying: maximum of 20%.
• Application of product: Apply the YHPrimBlock-1400 diluted according to requirements, on the substrate
in two coats with a brush, roller or with an airless spray system. If the product is applied on particularly hot
substrates, the said substrate may be dampened slightly. Apply the second coat perpendicular to the first,
making sure that each coat is even. The second coat may be applied after approximately 4-6 hours.
• Consumption: (1) 250-300 g/m2 per coat on non-absorbent surface; (2) 400-500 g/m2 per coat on
absorbent surface; (3) 1.5 kg/m2 per mm of thickness for smoothing application.
• Cleaning: Clean all work tools with water before YHPrimBlock-1400 sets. Once it has set, cleaning may
only be carried out by mechanical means.

TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
During transportation, anti-freezing measures should be taken to prevent exposure to sunlight, rain,
compression, and collision, and to ensure the packaging remains intact and undamaged. It should be stored in
a cool, ventilated, and dry warehouse, protected from rain, frost, humidity and direct sunlight, and kept away
from fire sources. The storage temperatures should be 5-35℃. Under normal storage and transportation
conditions, the shelf life is 12 months.

PRECAUTIONS
• Don't apply YHPrimBlock-1400 directly on surface with standing water or dusty, flaky, irregular surfaces
with bleeding.
• Don't apply YHPrimBlock-1400 on cracks which are subject to movement, as the product is rigid and may
also crack.

HEALTH AND SAFETY
The product must be handled properly. Users shall refer to the latest Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)
containing physical, ecological, toxicological and other safety-related data for information and advice on safe
handling, storage and disposal of chemicals.
