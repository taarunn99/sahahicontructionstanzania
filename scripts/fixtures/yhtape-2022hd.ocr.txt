YHTape-2022HD
Butyl Tape
Technical Date Sheet (TDS) 1/2

DESCRIPTION
YHTape-2022HD Butyl tape is a self-adhesive sealing waterproof tape composed of High Density
Polyethylene (HDPE) film surface layer, and a highly aggressive butyl pressure-sensitive adhesive on the
bottom side. This adhesive is specifically formulated to bond securely with various substrates, such as
concrete, steel, plywood or even glass, protecting critical detail areas from air, water, and wind infiltration
across all climates. Applications include sealing cracks, termination for waterproofing system, lapping, all
kinds of joints and etc.

WHERE TO USE
Sealing various kinds of material, such as concrete, plywood, steel, glass, and etc.
Non-roof detailing area, such as window and door openings, corner boards, wall-to-wall tie-ins, sheathing
panel seams, deck-to-wall intersections, foundation/sill capillary break.
Municipal drainage and sewage treatment system, such as sanitary manhole joints, storm-water manhole
joints, wet walls, and storm-water treatment & inlet structures.
Buildings and substructures waterproofing, such as architectural foundation detailing areas, underground
utility vaults.

ADVANTAGES
Superior adhesion—The tape bonds firmly to substrates and forms high quality laps.
Dual barrier protection—The lamination of butyl rubber and HDPE laminated film creates a continuous
barrier against water, air and vapor barrier.
Heat Resistance—Specially formulated, the tape withstands prolonged exposure to temperatures up to
100 °C without degradation of the butyl adhesive.
Superior flexibility at low temperature—Maintains good flexibility even at -40 °C.
Watertightness around nail shank—The unique butyl rubber based adhesive as the base material has
excellent sealing properties and can heal nail shank damage with a diameter of less than 2mm.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Substrate preparation: The substrate surface must be thoroughly cleaned and shall be solid, flat, dry,
and free of debris, dust, dirt, loose nails, or old roofing materials. Any protrusions on the deck must be
removed. Ensure the deck is free of voids, damaged areas, or unsupported sections. All such defective
areas shall be repaired prior to tape installation.
Tape installation: Peel back the release liner partially, align the tape with the target area, and continue
removing the release liner gradually as you advance. Press the tape firmly into position using heavy hand
pressure to ensure proper adhesion and eliminate air bubbles.

TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
The product should be stored in a cool, well-ventilated, and dry warehouse, protected from rain, sunlight,
and fire sources.
The storage temperature should be between 5°C and 35°C.
Under normal storage and transportation conditions, the shelf life is 24 months.

PRECAUTIONS
The base surface must be dry during construction. Construction is strictly prohibited on rainy or snowy
days, as well as on windy days with wind speeds exceeding level 5.
If the construction temperature is below 5°C, initial adhesion may be insufficient.
