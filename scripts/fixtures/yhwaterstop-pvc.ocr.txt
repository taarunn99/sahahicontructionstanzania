YHWaterstop
Flexible PVC Waterstop
YUHONG
Technical Date Sheet (TDS) 1 / 3
DESCRIPTION
YHWaterstop series are constructed from flexible thermoplastic PVC. They are designed to stop the migration of water through construction joints in concrete structures.
WHERE TO USE
YHWaterstop V series is suitable for the effective sealing of concrete construction joints in structures, such as water reservoirs, sewage treatment plants, swimming pools, basements, retaining walls, lift shafts, tunnels, culverts, etc.
YHWaterstop O series is suitable for the effective sealing of concrete expansion joints in structures, such as water reservoirs, sewage treatment plants, swimming pools, basements, retaining walls, lift shafts, tunnels, culverts, etc.
YHWaterstop A series is suitable for the effective sealing of concrete construction joints in structures, such as water reservoirs, sewage treatment plants, swimming pools, basements, retaining walls, lift shafts, tunnels, culverts, etc.
YHWaterstop D series is suitable for the effective sealing of concrete expansion joints in structures, such as water reservoirs, sewage treatment plants, swimming pools, basements, retaining walls, lift shafts, tunnels, culverts, etc.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Fixing to formwork
The "V" profile YHWaterstop V is fitted into the split formwork or shuttering for casting centrally into the stopends. It is used for construction joints and movement joints where nominal movement is anticipated, such as basement or retaining walls.
Fixing to reinforcement
Pre-punched eyelets are located in the outer flanges of the profiles. These simplify the fixing of waterstops to the steel reinforcement with tie wires to ensure the waterstops are not displaced during concreting.
Placing concrete first stage
YHWaterstop V performs its function only if both sides are well embedded in the concrete. Avoid formation of honey combs by vibrating carefully. The consistency of the concrete itself should be neither too plastic nor too stiff, and the aggregate must be well graded.
Placing of fresh concrete near the YHWaterstop V requires care, as otherwise it will be forced from its position by the pressure of the fresh concrete, i.e. the ends will fold up. To prevent this, the same concrete pressure must be present on both sides of the Waterstop.
Placing concrete second stage
Removal of formwork in the neighborhood of YHWaterstop V must be done with care. The end of the YHWaterstop V should be thoroughly checked for honeycombing on the stop-end and repaired if necessary.

Welding
On site welding can be undertaken using a electric welding knife. Both ends of the joint are heated simultaneously on the faces of the welding knife until an even, molten bead of PVC appears. The joint should be held rigid until the plastic cools down and solidifies. Check for any gaps or imperfect joints. Redo the welding if necessary. Failures can be caused by irregularities of cut edges, insufficient heat, dust etc.
TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
The product should be stored in ventilated places, with no exposure to sunlight, rain, or fire. The storage temperature should be between 5 °C and 30 °C. The shelf life is at least 12 months under normal transportation and storage conditions.
