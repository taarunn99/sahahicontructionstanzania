YHTape-2022FL
Fluorocarbon Film Butyl Tape

DESCRIPTION
YHTape-2022FL Fluorocarbon film butyl tape is a weather-resistant, self-adhesive waterproof sealing tape.
It consists of a surface layer made of fluorocarbon film, backed by a highly aggressive butyl-based
pressure-sensitive adhesive. This adhesive is specially formulated to form a secure bond with various
substrates, protecting critical details from air, water, and wind infiltration in all climates. Applications include
sealing cracks, terminating waterproofing systems, providing lap joints, and serving as a protective,
weather-resistant layer.

WHERE TO USE
Seal for the lap edge of exposed waterproofing membrane;
Provide protection for the all kind of roof detailing areas;
Provide protection for non-roof detailing area, such as window and door openings, corner boards,
wall-to-wall tie-ins, sheathing panel seams, deck-to-wall intersections, foundation/sill capillary break.

ADVANTAGES
Fluorocarbon weathering film has excellent weather resistance and can be used for exposed areas for
more than 15 years.
Reflective surface film—The white fluorocarbon film significantly reflects solar radiation energy, capable
of reducing roof temperature by about 10°C ~ 35°C in hot climates, thereby reducing cooling energy
consumption.
Superior adhesion—The tape bonds firmly to substrates and forms high quality laps.
Heat Resistance—Specially formulated, the tape withstands prolonged exposure to temperatures up to
100℃ without degradation of the butyl adhesive.
Superior flexibility at low temperature—Maintains good flexibility even at -40°C.
Water tightness around nail shank—The unique butyl rubber based adhesive as the base material has

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Substrate preparation: The substrate surface must be thoroughly cleaned and shall be solid, flat, dry,
and free of debris, dust, dirt, loose nails, or old roofing materials. Any protrusions on the deck must be
removed. Ensure the deck is free of voids, damaged areas, or unsupported sections. All such defective
areas shall be repaired prior to tape installation.
Tape installation: Peel back the release liner partially, align the tape with the target area, and continue
removing the release liner gradually as you advance. Press the tape firmly into position using heavy hand
pressure to ensure proper adhesion and eliminate air bubbles.

TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
The product should be stored in a cool, well-ventilated, and dry warehouse, protected from rain, sunlight,
and fire sources.
The storage temperature should be between 5°C and 35°C.
Under normal storage and transportation conditions, the shelf life is 24 months.

PRECAUTIONS
The base surface must be dry during construction. Construction is strictly prohibited on rainy or snowy
days, as well as on windy days with wind speeds exceeding level 5.
If the construction temperature is below 5°C, initial adhesion may be insufficient.
