YHPrimer-1000
Two-Component Water-Based Epoxy Primer

DESCRIPTION
YHPrimer-1000 is a two-component, water-based, rigid epoxy primer with both penetrative sealing and
surface-leveling effects. It is designed to enhance the adhesion of polyurethane waterproof coatings with
substrates and block micro-voids in porous substrates to mitigate out-gassing. It can be diluted with water to
a suitable viscosity according to the actual condition of the substrate.

WHERE TO USE
YHPrimer-1000 is suitable for concrete surfaces, compatible with moisture-cured and chemically-cured
polyurethane waterproof membranes, and water-based coatings, except for two-component hot spray
polyurea.

APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
Consumption Rate
The consumption rate is highly influenced by the absorption and roughness of the substrate and by the
application method used. Considering a homogeneous substrate, the consumption rate is approximately
0.15-0.25kg/m2. The primer can be diluted with 100-300% water by weight. The dilution rate depends on
substrate conditions: the more porous substrates and higher moisture contents requires a low dilution rate.
The coverage varies with the dilution rate.
Dilution rate / YHPrimer-1000 : Water | 1:1 | 1:2 | 1:3
Coverage / m2/set | 120-200 | 180-300 | 240-400

Substrate Preparation
Check the condition of the substrate prior to primer application. Existing holes must be repaired. Clean the
substrate by removing dirt, dust, grease, and laitance. For damp mortar, concrete, or ceramic substrates,
ensure that there is no standing water on the surface.

Mixing
The mix ratio is 5:3 (Part A: Part B) by weight. Add Part B into Part A and mix with a low-speed mechanical
mixer at low speed for at least 2 minutes with electric mixer fitted with a mixing attachment at low-speed to
avoid entraining air into the product until they are completely blended. It is not recommended to break down
the kits into smaller quantities. After proper mixing of the two components, 1-3 times of clean water may be
added to the mixture to adjust consistency. The pot life may extend after diluting with water, the pot life refer
to the below table after dilution.

Apply the primer by brush or roller at the recommended rate. The appropriate amount of primer should
saturate the substrate and leave a thin, continuous film on the top surface of the substrate. Be aware of the
out-gassing effect on porous substrates when the temperature rises. The primer should be applied as single
or double coat on the entire substrate using roller or brush.
Wait until completely dry before applying the next coating product; temperatures and environmental
conditions affect drying times. It normally takes about 6 hours for the primer to become tack-free at 25°C
(when diluted 3 times with water). Lower temperatures will extend the waiting time. It is highly
recommended to apply the next coating product within 24 hours after the primer becomes tack-free. A
longer gap between the primer and the next coating product may result in deteriorated interface adhesion
and failure.

TRANSPORTATION AND STORAGE
During transportation and storage, please protect the product from sunlight and rain, keep it away from fire
sources, and avoid collisions. Store it in a well-ventilated place. The recommended storage temperature
range is 5°C to 35°C. Do not store the product above 40°C. The shelf life is 12 months under the
recommended storage conditions.
