# 04 - Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Zanzibar and the southern neighbours (Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, DRC)

Research date: 2026-11-05
Prepared for: Sahihi Construction Chemicals Limited (sahihicc.com) website build
Scope: regional SERP record, who sells Mapei in each country, competitor web strength, trade-corridor reality, geo-target grading, and a regional page architecture.

---

## 0. Method and limits (read this first)

- **Search tool, not Google.** The 18 SERP records below come from the WebSearch tool, which is US-based and returns a blended list of links. It is not google.co.ug, google.rw or google.co.tz, it is not localised, and its ordering is not a Google rank. Treat every "position" as "order returned by the tool on 2026-11-05", nothing more. No ranking in this document is a Google ranking.
- **Search budget ran out.** The session shares 200 searches across 14 agents. This agent got 22 searches through (all 18 required queries plus 4 extra) before the budget was exhausted. Follow-up verification searches (for example for Sapio Building Solutions, a Rwanda Mapei seller, a Zambia Mapei distributor) could **not** be run. Everything after that point was done by fetching known or guessed URLs directly.
- **Blocked sources.** mapei.com (all country folders) returns HTTP 403 to our fetcher, so statements attributed to Mapei pages come from search-result summaries and from trade press, not from reading the Mapei page itself. LinkedIn returned HTTP 999. jiji.ug, jiji.co.tz, ubuy.ug, ubuy.com.zm, monitor.co.ug, stoneworld.com and adhesivesmag.com returned 403. Those are marked where used.
- **No keyword-volume data.** No Ahrefs/Semrush/Keyword Planner access in this session. EASY / MEDIUM / HARD grades are reasoned judgments from who appears in the results and how strong their pages are. They are not measured difficulty scores.
- **Site audits are light.** Audits are from one or two fetched pages per site plus a raw HTML check (title, meta description, generator tag, JSON-LD types, hreflang). No crawl, no Core Web Vitals, no backlink data.
- **"Not verified"** means no source was found in this session. It does not mean the thing is false.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Mapei's official East Africa vehicle is Mapei East Africa Ltd, Nairobi**, announced December 2018 to develop "the markets in Kenya and surrounding countries" (https://tunnelingonline.com/mapei-extends-international-presence-to-east-africa/). Its old domain mapei.co.ke now 301-redirects to the UAE site https://www.mapei.com/ae/en/home-page (observed by HTTP header check, 2026-11-05). There is no Mapei country website for Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi or Tanzania that we could find.
2. **Uganda may already have a named Mapei reseller.** The search tool surfaced a LinkedIn post describing "Sapio Building Solutions Ltd" as the Mapei reseller in Uganda with a dedicated Mapei showroom in Kampala (surfaced via https://eg.linkedin.com/in/rafaelmagdy; LinkedIn itself returned HTTP 999 so the post was not read directly). **Single source, not verified.** No Sapio website was found at seven guessed domains. Treat "Mapei Uganda" as possibly locked until the owner confirms with Mapei.
3. **Rwanda, Burundi, Zanzibar: no Mapei seller found at all.** The results for "Mapei Rwanda", "Mapei Kigali", "Mapei Burundi" and "Mapei Zanzibar" are Wikipedia pages, corporate profiles and unrelated noise. This is a content vacuum, but a vacuum in search results is not proof that nobody holds the territory.
4. **Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, DRC sit in Mapei South Africa's stated patch.** Mapei South Africa has been a subsidiary since 2009 and, per the search summary of its own page, distributes throughout the SADC region (https://www.mapei.com/za/en/about-us/mapei-in-southern-africa, page itself 403 to us). The only Mapei sellers surfaced for Zambia and Malawi were Ubuy cross-border retail listings (https://www.ubuy.com.zm/product/1NKGIIGG-mapei-ultracolor-plus-fa-rapid-setting-grout-25-lbs-all-colors-frost-77, https://www.ubuy.mw/product/19USKCZMY-mapei-ultracolor-plus-fa-rapid-setting-grout-25lbs-all-colors-chocolate-07). No appointed local distributor found (not verified).
5. **Dar es Salaam port genuinely is the gateway for the south and west, much less so for Uganda.** 2015/16 split of non-Tanzanian traffic through the port: Zambia 37.5%, DR Congo 30.3%, Rwanda 17.4%, Burundi 7.27%, Uganda 3.23%, Malawi 2.21% (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_of_Dar_es_Salaam, figures are old). Delivery claims to Zambia, DRC, Rwanda and Burundi are logistically plausible. Uganda is mainly a Mombasa / Northern Corridor market (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Corridor).
6. **X-Calibur already has its own Kenya-based "East Africa" company**: X-Calibur Construction Chemistry EA Limited, Old Mombasa Road, Nairobi, site titled "... | East Africa" (https://www.x-calibur.co.ke/, https://www.x-calibur.ae/contact-us). Sahihi's X-Calibur rights outside Tanzania need written confirmation before any Uganda/Rwanda copy mentions X-Calibur.
7. **Sika, the strongest multinational competitor, has country sites for Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique, Ethiopia and South Africa, but none for Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Zambia, Malawi or DRC** (hreflang list read from https://tza.sika.com/ on 2026-11-05). In those six countries the organic field is local resellers and directories with weak sites.
8. **Local competitor sites in Uganda and Rwanda are weak.** Examples: Lakes Construction Support (Kampala) sends every product link to one page and has no structured data (https://www.lakescs.com/); Builders Industries (Kigali, "main authorized distributor of BASF/SIKA") had a website returning HTTP 500 on 2026-11-05 (https://www.buildersindustries.rw/, description from https://www.jobinrwanda.com/employer/builders-industries-ltd).
9. **Zanzibar has real demand drivers and no specialist ranking.** 917,000 tourist arrivals in 2025; 124,481 in August 2026 with estimated bed occupancy of 92.9% (https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/zanzibar, https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/tourism/zanzibar-international-tourist-arrivals-august-2026). "waterproofing Zanzibar" returned Airbnb, Jiji classifieds and unrelated pages.
10. **sahihicc.com is currently a Namecheap parking page** (title "Namecheap Parking Page", fetched 2026-11-05). There is no existing equity to protect and no redirects to plan from the live domain.

### 3.2 Mapei East Africa Ltd (the entity that matters most)

- Announced 7 December 2018 as Mapei's 87th subsidiary, in Nairobi. Quote: "Mapei East Africa Ltd will be focused on developing the markets in Kenya and surrounding countries", "an area having almost 300 million citizens". Led by Stefano Iannacone, Middle East and East Africa Regional Director. Source: https://tunnelingonline.com/mapei-extends-international-presence-to-east-africa/
- Current listing per the search summary of Mapei's global locations page: Tatu City, Kiambu, Nairobi, phone +254 709 045 000 (https://www.mapei.com/ca/en-ca/about-us/Our-Global-Locations?continent=africa - page 403 to us, taken from the search summary).
- Web presence: mapei.co.ke returns "301 Moved Permanently" to https://www.mapei.com/ae/en/home-page (header check 2026-11-05). So Mapei's East Africa web footprint is folded into the UAE site. No distributor list for the region was found.
- Implication: Mapei itself does not publish country landing pages for Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi or Tanzania. A properly authorised distributor page is the best available answer for those queries.

### 3.3 Territory risk (important for copy)

- Tanzania is a member of both the EAC and SADC (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_African_Community , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_African_Development_Community). Mapei appears to split Africa between Mapei East Africa (Kenya and neighbours) and Mapei South Africa (SADC). Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique and probably DRC fall on the South African side.
- A Tanzanian distributor writing "Mapei distributor Zambia" or "Mapei distributor Uganda" could be claiming someone else's territory. That is a contract and trademark problem, not just an SEO one.
- Safe wording pattern until the owner has it in writing: "Authorized Mapei distributor in Tanzania. We supply project orders to customers in [country] from Dar es Salaam" - and only for countries the owner confirms.

## 8. Recommended regional architecture (not a doorway farm)

**Principle.** One page per real thing: a brand, a solution, a product, a project, a place Sahihi actually delivers to. Never a place multiplied by a category.

```
/                                   Home (Tanzania-first)
/brands/mapei/                      "Mapei in Tanzania - authorized distributor" (territory stated exactly)
/brands/oriental-yuhong/  /brands/x-calibur/  /brands/cmci/  /brands/bihui/
/solutions/waterproofing/           Category hubs (7)
/solutions/tile-adhesives-grouts/   ...
/products/{product-slug}/           One page per product, TDS download, WhatsApp enquiry
/projects/{project-slug}/           Case studies, each tagged with city and country
/where-we-supply/                   ONE regional hub: map, corridor table, how cross-border orders work
/where-we-supply/zanzibar/          Launch page (home territory, real demand)
/where-we-supply/rwanda/            Only after owner confirms supply + brand rights
/where-we-supply/zambia/            Only after owner confirms; mining-led; no Mapei claim unless cleared
/fr/rdc/ (or /where-we-supply/drc/) Only if owner serves Katanga/Kivu; French
/guides/...                         Informational: e.g. importing construction chemicals via Dar es Salaam
```

**Rules that keep it honest.**

1. **A country page exists only if it can carry at least four unique blocks**: a real delivered project or named customer type in that country; the actual route, border post and typical lead time; documents and payment terms for that country; which brands Sahihi is allowed to sell there. If it cannot, the country is a section on /where-we-supply/.
2. **No country x category pages** (/waterproofing-uganda/, /tile-adhesive-rwanda/). Category hubs get a short "Delivered across Tanzania and to confirmed neighbouring countries" block linking to /where-we-supply/.
3. **No foreign city pages** (Kampala, Kigali, Lusaka, Lubumbashi). Cities are mentioned inside the country page.
4. **Tanzanian cities**: one section each on /where-we-supply/ at launch. Promote a city to its own page only when there is a branch, stockist, or two or more projects there. Zanzibar qualifies first; Dodoma, Arusha, Mwanza and Mbeya are next candidates on the evidence in section 6.
5. **Brand claims are per country.** The Mapei logo and "authorized distributor" wording appear on a country page only where the owner holds that right in writing. Elsewhere the page talks about solutions and the brands that are cleared.
6. **Structured data**: Organization / LocalBusiness with the Dar es Salaam address; areaServed limited to confirmed places; Product markup without price; BreadcrumbList. None of the audited regional competitors except Sika has more than default schema.
7. **Languages**: English site. No hreflang needed for English-only country pages (hreflang is for language/region alternates of the same page, which these are not). Add French only for a real DRC/Burundi offer; Swahili for Tanzania is a separate decision.
8. **Lead capture**: WhatsApp links pre-filled with the country and solution so the owner can see which regions actually produce enquiries before investing in more pages.
9. **Review gate**: after 6 months, keep or expand a country page only if Search Console shows impressions from that country and at least one enquiry came from it.
