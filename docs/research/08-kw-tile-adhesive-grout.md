## 1. Method and limits (read first)

**What was done**

- 24 live web searches were run, then the session's shared search budget (200 calls across all 14 agents) ran out. The brief asked for 40+ searches and 30+ SERP checks. That target was NOT met: 13 target queries have a real SERP behind them, plus 11 competitor / brand / site-restricted searches. Everything else in this file is marked "provisional" and is inferred from the adjacent SERPs, not observed.
- After the budget ran out, about 45 direct page fetches were made against known primary sources (Sika Tanzania, Sika Kenya, CTM Tanzania, KEBS draft standard, Tanzanian and Kenyan news, TAL, Taha Tiles). No search engine was queried by other means.
- PDFs from Sika Tanzania, Sika Kenya and KEBS were opened and read directly.

**Limits that affect decisions**

1. **No search volumes.** There is no volume tool. Demand is validated only by SERP composition (who ranks, what page type) and by marketplace evidence. No volume figures appear anywhere in this file.
2. **The search tool is US-located.** Results approximate, but are not identical to, google.co.tz or google.co.ke. Local-pack / map results, ads and local ordering were not visible.
3. **People Also Ask was not captured.** The search tool returns result links only, no PAA box. Section 6 therefore contains NO verbatim PAA. It lists (a) questions and headings that really appear on ranking or competitor pages, with URLs, and (b) candidate questions clearly labelled as unverified. PAA needs a manual check in a Tanzanian browser session.
4. **Blocked sources.** mapei.com (all country sites tried), jiji.co.tz, jiji.co.ke, ctm.co.ke, idealceramics.com, tacc.co.ke, saint-gobain-africa.com, nation.africa and capitalfm.africa returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch; tbs.go.tz PDF links returned 404. For those, only the SERP title / snippet was seen. Each such case is flagged "SERP snippet only".
5. **Mapei product classes** (Mapeset C1, Kerabond T C1T, Adesilex P7 C2T, Keraflex Maxi S1 C2TE S1, Keracolor FF CG2WA) are taken from the project brief, not re-verified against Mapei data sheets, because mapei.com blocked fetching. Verify each against the current Mapei TDS before publishing.
6. **Swahili usage is largely unverified.** See section 7. No Swahili-language page about tile adhesive was found for any Swahili query tested.

**Baseline fact:** sahihicc.com currently serves a Namecheap parking page ("Your website is ready to go!"), so there is no existing content, ranking or URL equity to protect. Source: https://sahihicc.com/ (fetched 2026-11-05).

## 2. Headline findings

1. **The Tanzanian tile-adhesive SERP has no real owner.** For "tile adhesive Tanzania", "tile adhesive Dar es Salaam", "tile adhesive suppliers in Tanzania" and "tile adhesive price in Tanzania", the results are TBS standards PDFs, tradebarriers.org regulation records, Jiji category pages, market-research stubs, and South African / Kenyan retailers. No Tanzanian supplier page built around the topic appeared in any of them. Sources: SERPs in section 5.
2. **"Mapei tile adhesive Tanzania" returns zero Tanzanian pages** - only mapei.com/za, buco.co.za, leroymerlin.co.za, toolstation.com and indiamart. Sahihi can own its own brand-plus-country queries almost by default. Source: SERP row 6, section 5.
3. **Sika is the real technical competitor in Tanzania, and its locally documented range is mostly C1T.** Sika Tanzania's data sheets state "C1T classified according to EN 12004" for SikaCeram-105 Multi TZ and SikaCeram-108 Extra TZ; SikaCeram-80 TZ cites no class at all and is limited to internal horizontal use. Sika also lists SikaCeram-205 Super TZ (class not verified here). Sahihi's C2T (Adesilex P7) and C2TE S1 (Keraflex Maxi S1) are a genuine class advantage for porcelain and large format. Sources: https://tza.sika.com/dms/getdocument.get/c7e6e431-9940-4a8e-92af-ca1dc0d7f3be/sikaceram-105-multitz.pdf , https://tza.sika.com/dam/dms/tz01/f/sikaceram-108-extratz.pdf , https://tza.sika.com/dam/dms/tz01/j/sikaceram-80-tz.pdf , https://tza.sika.com/en/construction/72233/tile-setting-systems.html
4. **Sika Tanzania has no FAQ, no blog and no calculator** on its tile pages - only a category page with five application system guides and a brochure. CTM Tanzania's adhesive and grout category pages have no SEO text or FAQ either. The informational layer is empty locally. Sources: https://tza.sika.com/en/construction/72233/tile-setting-systems.html , https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-adhesives.html , https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-grouts.html
5. **Public price anchors exist and Sahihi will be compared against them.** CTM Tanzania lists Pro Grip Kilimanjaro 25 kg at TSh 39,000 and Pro Grip Superbond 25 kg at TSh 41,000; grouts at TSh 25,500 - 26,000 per 5 kg. Sahihi's no-price policy means its pages must sell on class, coverage and cost per square metre, not bag price. Sources: https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-adhesives.html , https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-grouts.html
6. **Epoxy grout is an open lane.** CTM Tanzania offers no epoxy grout. The "epoxy grout Tanzania price" SERP is Jiji and Desertcart listings; a Kerapoxy 5 kg listing was reported on Jiji Kenya at KSh 10,500 (SERP snippet only). Sources: https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-grouts.html , https://jiji.co.ke/nairobi/165-tile/grout
7. **Kenya is harder.** "tile adhesive Kenya" and Nairobi price queries are held by priced e-commerce (ctm.co.ke, idealceramics.com, tacc.co.ke, jiji.co.ke), manufacturer PDFs and national news. Treat Kenya as a secondary, product-name-led play. Source: SERP rows 12-13, section 5.
8. **A possible stock gap for one requested query.** "Cement residue remover" is normally an acid-cleaner job. Whether UltraCare HD Cleaner covers it could not be verified (mapei.com blocked). Confirm against the Mapei TDS before assigning that query to HD Cleaner; if it does not, either stock the right product or do not target the query commercially. Source: none - flagged as unverified.

## 8. Local specificity

### 8.1 Climate facts that should shape the copy

- Dar es Salaam has a tropical savanna climate, mean daily maximum 31.2 C across the year (32.8 C in February), average relative humidity 79 percent (84 percent in April), about 1,150 mm of rain, with long rains in April - May and short rains in November - December. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dar_es_Salaam
- A locally issued adhesive data sheet sets working limits of substrate temperature max +35 C and ambient max +40 C, gives open time as 20 minutes at 25 C, and warns to "protect freshly applied material from rapid drying due to heat, wind". Source: https://tza.sika.com/dam/dms/tz01/j/sikaceram-80-tz.pdf
- Inference (ours, not a sourced claim): with normal daily highs of 31 - 33 C, sun-exposed slabs in Dar es Salaam will often sit at or above the top of that working window, and open time measured at 25 C will be shorter on site. This is the practical argument for extended-open-time (E) adhesives such as Keraflex Maxi S1, and for the "tenting in heat" and "movement joints" content. Confirm the wording with Mapei technical support before publishing.
- Coastal care is already a topic a Zanzibar tile showroom writes about ("Caring for Your Tiles and Fixtures in Coastal Environments"), which supports a coastal angle on tile-care and grout content. Source: https://tahatilesandsanitary.co.tz/news-blogs

### 8.2 Tiling practice

- **Sand-cement bedding.** No Tanzania-specific published source was found that quantifies how common sand-cement (thick bed) fixing still is. Indirect evidence: (a) a Kenyan national newspaper ran a feature framed exactly as "Tile adhesives or sand-cement mixture?" (https://nation.africa/kenya/life-and-style/dn2/tile-adhesives-or-sand-cement-mixture--3819924); (b) Sika Tanzania feels the need to label its entry product "for thin Bed Application" (https://tza.sika.com/dam/dms/tz01/j/sikaceram-80-tz.pdf); (c) Bamburi launched its adhesive in 2022 citing "the prevalence of popping out, cracking and falling of tiles" (https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2022-03-30-bamburi-cement-enters-mortar-sector-with-tile-adhesive); (d) "Tile Hollow Sealant" repair products are widely listed on Jiji Tanzania (SERP snippet only, https://jiji.co.tz/165-tile/waterproof). Treat "sand-cement is still common in Tanzania" as field knowledge to be confirmed by Sahihi's sales team, not as a cited fact.
- **Large format and porcelain.** A Zanzibar showroom lists tiles up to 80x160 alongside 60x60 (https://tahatilesandsanitary.co.tz/). Sika Tanzania leads its application guides with "Tiling with Large-Format Ceramic" (https://tza.sika.com/en/construction/72233/tile-setting-systems.html). CTM Tanzania's two bagged adhesives are both sold as "Porcelain Floor and Wall Tile Adhesive" (https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-adhesives.html). Regional Weber guidance says to use a flexible adhesive for porcelain larger than 60x60 cm and to double-butter tiles above 900 cm2 (SERP snippet only, https://www.saint-gobain-africa.com/en/assets/view/media/11661). No market-size figure for large-format growth in Tanzania was found.
- **Local tile supply.** Twyford Tanzania runs a tile factory at Chalinze, about 70 km from Dar es Salaam, with close to 1,000 employees, selling across Tanzania including Dodoma and Zanzibar and exporting to eight countries. Source: https://dailynews.co.tz/?p=138662 . Goodwill Tanzania Ceramic's Mkuranga plant was announced at 80,000 m2 of tiles per day. Source: https://tanzaniainvest.com/?p=12370 . More locally made tile means more tiling jobs that need adhesive.

### 8.3 Standards language buyers see

- **Tanzania - current.** TZS 1434-1:2011 "Ceramic tiles - Grouts and adhesives - Part 1: Terms, definitions and specifications for adhesives", issued by TBS, implemented 28 July 2011, covering cementitious (C), dispersion (D) and reaction resin (R) adhesives for internal and external walls and floors. Source: https://tradebarriers.org/ntm/regulations/view/1200
- **Tanzania - in progress.** tbs.go.tz hosts documents titled DTZS ISO 13007-1, -2, -3, -4, -5 and DTZS ISO 14448:2016 (low-modulus adhesives for exterior tile finishing). The file names carry timestamps that decode to about March 2026, which suggests TBS is adopting the ISO 13007 series now. The PDFs returned 404 on direct fetch, so contents and status are NOT confirmed - verify with TBS. Sources (SERP snippet only): https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1774357770-BCDC%209%20(3854)%20DTZS%20ISO%2013007%201.pdf ; https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1774358164-BCDC%209%20(4231)%20DTZS%20ISO%2014448%202016.pdf
- **Kenya.** FDKS 2129-1:2023 (2nd edition, public review draft) "Tile adhesive for marble, granite, ceramic and porcelain - Part 1", prepared with reference to EN 12004:2007. Read directly. It gives the same code system East African buyers see on Mapei and Sika bags. Source: https://members.wto.org/crnattachments/2023/TBT/KEN/23_09870_00_e.pdf

Code meanings as written in the KEBS draft (same source):

| Code | Meaning | Requirement in the draft |
|---|---|---|
| C | Cementitious adhesive | - |
| C1 | Normal setting cementitious adhesive | Tensile adhesion strength >= 0.5 N/mm2 (initial, after water immersion, after heat ageing, after freeze-thaw) |
| C2 | Improved (additional characteristics) | Tensile adhesion strength >= 1 N/mm2 under the same four conditions |
| T | Reduced slip | Slip <= 0.5 mm |
| E | Extended open time | >= 0.5 N/mm2 after not less than 30 minutes |
| F | Fast setting | >= 0.5 N/mm2 after not more than 6 hours |
| S1 | Deformable | Transverse deformation >= 2.5 mm and < 5 mm |
| S2 | Highly deformable | Transverse deformation >= 5 mm |
| D, R | Dispersion and reaction resin adhesives | Separate tables |

- **How competitors use the codes locally.** Sika prints "C1T classified according to EN 12004" on its Tanzanian and Kenyan sheets. Sources: https://tza.sika.com/dam/dms/tz01/f/sikaceram-108-extratz.pdf ; https://ken.sika.com/dam/dms/ke01/q/sikaceram-200.pdf
- **Grout codes (EN 13888 CG2WA).** No fetchable source for the EN 13888 code definitions was obtained in this session. The brief states Keracolor FF is CG2WA. Take the exact definitions of CG2, W and A from the Mapei Keracolor FF data sheet or the standard itself before publishing; do not rely on this file for them.

Copy implication: buyers in both markets already see EN 12004 codes on competing bags, and the regulators use the same code system. A plain-language "what C1, C2, T, E, S1 mean" block on the pillar, tied to which Mapei product carries which code, is on-language for this market.

## 11. Interactive tool recommendation

**Build one tool: "Tile adhesive selector and quantity calculator" (adhesive class + bags + grout), ending in a pre-filled WhatsApp message.**

Why this one
- No Tanzanian competitor page seen has a calculator: Sika Tanzania has none and CTM Tanzania has none. Sources: https://tza.sika.com/en/construction/72233/tile-setting-systems.html ; https://www.ctm.co.tz/tiles/tile-essentials/tile-adhesives.html
- The closest regional reference, TAL's grout calculator, has no lead capture - it gives a number and stops. Source: https://tal.co.za/calculators/grout-calculator
- It turns Sahihi's real advantage (a full class ladder from C1 to C2TE S1, versus a mostly C1T local Sika range) into something a buyer experiences rather than reads.
- Sahihi shows no prices, so the natural next step after "you need 14 bags of X" is "send this list on WhatsApp for a quote".

Inputs
1. Tile type: ceramic / porcelain / natural stone / mosaic.
2. Tile size: preset buttons (30x30, 30x60, 60x60, 60x120, 80x80, 80x160) plus custom length and width.
3. Where: interior floor / interior wall / bathroom or wet area / balcony or exterior / swimming pool.
4. Substrate: cement screed / concrete / plaster / existing tiles.
5. Area in m2.
6. Optional: joint width and tile thickness (for grout); waste percentage (default to be set).

Outputs
1. Recommended class and Mapei product (for example C2TE S1 - Keraflex Maxi S1), with one line of why.
2. Trowel notch suggestion and whether back-buttering is advised.
3. Adhesive kg and number of 25 kg bags.
4. Grout kg and packs, using the standard joint-volume method (the same inputs TAL uses: tile length, width, thickness, joint width, grout density, area, waste).
5. Button: "Send this list on WhatsApp" with the inputs and results pre-filled, plus an email-enquiry fallback.

Data needed before build (do not ship with placeholder numbers)
- Consumption per m2 by notch size for each Mapei adhesive, from the Mapei data sheets (mapei.com blocked fetching in this session, so these are NOT in this file).
- Sanity-check ranges from competitor sheets, for testing only: one 25 kg bag covers approximately 5 - 7 m2 at a 2 - 5 mm bed (https://tza.sika.com/dam/dms/tz01/j/sikaceram-80-tz.pdf); about 1.4 kg of powder per m2 per mm of thickness, with square-notch guidance of 4 - 6 mm for tiles up to 30 cm, 6 - 8 mm up to 45 cm and 8 - 10 mm up to 60 cm (https://gcc.sika.com/en/construction/building-finishing/tiling-system/tile-adhesive/sikaceram-100.html).
- The selection rules (which product for which tile size, location and substrate) must be signed off by Mapei technical support or Sahihi's technical lead. A wrong recommendation from a tool is a liability, so the tool should also show a "confirm with our technical team for pools, facades and tiles above X size" note.

SEO role: the tool page owns queries 57 - 60 and is the internal-link target from the pillar, every adhesive product page, and J1, J3 and J6.
