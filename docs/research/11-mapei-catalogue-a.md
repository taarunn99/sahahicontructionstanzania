# 11 - Mapei catalogue research, part A (ceramic, resilient and building lines) - 2026-11-05

Research for the Sahihi Construction Chemicals Limited catalogue website. Scope: 26 price-list items plus Adesilex P9. Research only, no content written for the site.

## Method and limits

- **Primary source for every technical fact: the official Mapei Technical Data Sheet (TDS) PDF, downloaded directly from Mapei's CDN (cdnmedia.mapei.com) and read in full as text.** Each item below names the TDS version code printed on the sheet.
- **Product pages: www.mapei.com returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare block) to every automated request**, for the UAE, Kenya and international sites alike. I did not try to get around that block. Product page content was instead read from `cms.mapei.com`, a public Mapei host that serves the same pages and whose canonical tags point to the `www.mapei.com` URLs listed here. Caveat: on that host the Safety Data Sheet row carries a "TEST TEST" label, so it may be an authoring front end and page text could differ slightly from the live www page. **Recommend a human opens each www.mapei.com URL once in a browser before publication.**
- **Mapei UAE (www.mapei.com/ae/en) is the reference site** because the ME grades are listed there. Where the UAE site had no page or no TDS, I used the Mapei Italy English site (it/en, international English TDS, code "en (IT)") and say so.
- **Mapei East Africa / Kenya (www.mapei.com/ke/en-ke): not reachable** (403 on www, 404 on cms). Nothing in this report is verified against the Kenya site. The `it/en-international` path was also not reachable.
- **Web search budget for the session ran out after three queries**, so there are no independent search cross-checks. Everything rests on Mapei's own pages and PDFs.
- "Classification" below repeats only what Mapei prints. Where a class appears only as a badge image on the page (read from the image file name) and not in the TDS text, that is stated.
- Mapei's en dashes are replaced by plain hyphens in quotes. Otherwise quotes are verbatim, including Mapei's own typos.
- Pack image URLs are the main pack shot on the product page. Image licensing was not checked.
- "Not verified" means I could not confirm it from a Mapei source.

URL shorthand used below:
- PAGE-AE = `https://www.mapei.com/ae/en/products-and-solutions/products/detail/`

# CERAMIC LINE

## 1. Mapeset - Grey

- **Official name:** Mapeset (page heading MAPESET). Price-list name matches. Colour is "grey" or "white".
- **Official description:** "Cement based adhesive for tiles" (TDS); page: "Cement-based adhesive for tiles."
- **Product line (mapei.com):** Installation of ceramics and natural stone > Adhesives > Standard cementitious adhesives.
- **Classification:** "Mapeset is a normal (1) cementitious (C) adhesive of class C1." In compliance with "European EN 12004 as C1", "International ISO 13007-1 as C1", "American ANSI A 118.1". EMICODE: "EC1 Plus - very low emission".
- **Where used:**
  - "Internal and external bonding of ceramic tiles and mosaics, single-fired and double-fired tiles for floors, walls and ceilings."
  - Substrates: traditional render or cement mortar; gypsum or anhydrite after Primer G or Eco Prim T Plus; cured, dry cementitious screeds.
  - Do not use: on wood, metal, rubber, PVC, linoleum; for glass mosaics; **"Large format tiles"**; on pre-cast concrete or surfaces subject to excessive movements.
- **Key technical data:** colour white or grey; water 24-26 parts per 100 (grey), 23-25 (white); pot life 6-8 hours; open time > 20 minutes; adjustability approx. 45 minutes; wall grouting after 4-8 hours, floor grouting after 24 hours; light foot traffic 24 hours; ready for use 14 days; application temperature +5 to +40 C.
- **Consumption:** trowel No. 4 approx. 2 kg/m2; trowel No. 5 (5x5 mm) 2.5-3 kg/m2; trowel No. 6 approx. 5 kg/m2.
- **Packaging (Mapei):** "white and grey 25 kg bags". Shelf life 12 months.
- **URLs:** page PAGE-AE`mapeset` | TDS (version 95-5-2023 (UAE)) DOC-AE`1_00095_mapeset_en-ae_570b20a991a04ad3b8dc8ecb4d4ae07d.pdf?sfvrsn=f52619d3_0` | image IMG-AE`4_mapeset-uae_323085b2d8364a9d962b14a1569a0cdc.png?sfvrsn=24a5a2e3_0`
- **Discrepancies vs price list:**
  - Consumption "3-5 kg/sqm size 5 notch": Mapei gives 2.5-3 kg/m2 for the No. 5 trowel. 5 kg/m2 is the No. 6 figure.
  - "ceramic/porcelain": the Mapeset TDS does not mention porcelain. It names ceramic tiles, mosaics, single-fired and double-fired tiles, and excludes large format tiles. Do not claim porcelain suitability.
  - Class C1 and 25 kg pack: match.

## 11. Primer G

- **Official name:** Primer G. Matches.
- **Official description (TDS):** "Synthetic resin primer in water dispersion with a very low content of volatile organic compounds (VOC)."
- **Product line:** Screeds and preparation of floor substrates > Adhesion promoter primers.
- **Classification:** EMICODE "EC1 Plus - very low emission"; VOC "0 g/l" (2004/42/EC). No EN class. Note: the UAE page body text says "EMICODE: EC1" while its badge and the TDS say EC1 Plus.
- **Where used:**
  - Treating gypsum surfaces before ceramic tiles.

# Notes for website content generation

**Spelling landmines**
- UltraCare (capital C), "UltraCare Multicleaner" (one word), "UltraCare HD Cleaner", "UltraCare Stain Protector W Plus".
- "Mapefibre NS 12", never "Mapefiber".
- "Triblock P", never "Primer Triblock P".
- "Mapegrout T60 ME" is not "Mapegrout T60"; "Mapegrout ME05" and "Mapegrout ME 06" are two products. Mapei itself writes ME05 / ME 05 and ME06 / ME 06 inconsistently, so pick the TDS title form and keep it consistent.
- "Ultraplan EC20" (current UAE name) vs "Ultraplan Eco 20" (price list and Egypt TDS).
- "Keraflex Maxi S1", "Adesilex PG2 TG", "Mapefer 1K", "Mapefix EP 50", "Epojet LV", "Eporip", "Planicrete SP", "Lignobond", "Kerapoxy", "Keracolor FF".
- Class codes are written without spaces: C1, C1T, C2T, C2TE, C2TES1, CG2WA, RG, R2T, R4, CT-C20-F5-A1FL.

**Never-invent warnings**
- Kerapoxy is RG / R2T. Never print CG2WA for it.
- No mould-resistance claim for Keracolor FF.
- No porcelain or large-format claim for Mapeset.
- No strength or structural claim for Mapefibre NS 12; no screed claim either.
- No seismic claim for Mapefix EP 50 (that belongs to EP 100).
- No EN 1504 class for Adesilex PG2 TG and no EN class for Triblock P, Primer G, Planicrete SP, Lignobond or the UltraCare products: Mapei states none in the sheets read.
- Kerabond T reaches C2ES2 only "if mixed with Isolastic". Never present C2ES2 as its own class.
- Coverage figures for the cleaners hold only at the stated dilution (1:20 for HD Cleaner, 1:200 for Multicleaner). Always print the dilution with the figure.
- Consumption for grouts (Keracolor FF, Kerapoxy) depends on tile size and joint width. Do not publish a single number.
- Liquids in the UltraCare range are sold in litres, not kg.
- Cure and strength values are Mapei "typical values" at +23 C and 50% R.H. Label them that way.
- Do not publish the scrambled values flagged above (Adesilex P9 secondary bond strengths, Epojet LV volumetric shrinkage).
- Several TDS editions exist per product (UAE, international, UK, Egypt) with different packs and sometimes different data. Cite the edition that matches the bag in the warehouse.
- Mapei's TDS legal notice says TDS content may be copied into project documents but any alteration of the wording excludes Mapei's responsibility. Quote exactly and link to the TDS.
