# 06 - Brand dossiers: X-Calibur Construction Chemistry and CMCI

Research date: 2026-11-05
Prepared for: Sahihi Construction Chemicals Limited (sahihicc.com), Dar es Salaam, Tanzania
Scope: brand identity, official product range, verification of the six X-Calibur price-list lines, certifications, Africa footprint, SERP record, brand-hub recommendations.

## Method and limits (read this first)

- Sources used: the manufacturers' own websites and PDFs (x-calibur.us, x-calibur.co.ke, cmci.com.sa), fetched directly on 2026-11-05; eleven X-Calibur Technical Data Sheet PDFs and two CMCI TDS PDFs downloaded and text-extracted; the X-Calibur "Product Menu" brochure PDF; one Kenyan newspaper article; and the owner-supplied files in `assets/source/owner-supplied/` (X-Calibur retail price list 2026 TZ, business card), which were read only.
- The X-Calibur TDS index was crawled programmatically (10 categories, 53 sub-categories, about 790 index entries, some near-duplicates) so that "no product of this name exists on the official site" statements are based on the full index, not on a sample.
- SERP limits: the web search tool is US-only (not google.co.tz or google.co.ke), returns an unranked link list rather than strict positions, and the session-wide search budget (shared by 14 agents) ran out part-way. Seven queries were actually run; the remaining requested queries were NOT run and are marked "not run" in the SERP section. Gradings for those are estimates from site evidence, not from an observed SERP.
- Not verified anywhere in this research: that Sahihi is an authorised distributor of either brand (neither manufacturer's site lists any Tanzanian distributor), trademark registration numbers (the Justia trademark page returned HTTP 403), ISO certificate numbers or certifying bodies for either brand, and CMCI's reported technical link with Euclid Chemical (trade press article now returns HTTP 404).
- Pages that could not be fetched: gulfindustryonline.com and gulfconstructiononline.com articles on CMCI (404), pressreader.com interview with CMCI's GM (403), x-calibur.co.za (connection refused), constructionkenya.com X-Calibur article (404), trademarks.justia.com (403).
- No prices are reproduced in this report. No browser automation was used.

### 1.3 The six price-list lines (detail)

Owner file read: `assets/source/owner-supplied/xcalibur-retail-pricelist-2026.pdf` (header "RETAIL PRICE LIST 2026 TZ", X-Calibur Construction Chemistry logo). Summary table is in the "Six-row product verification table" below; detail follows.

**Line 1 - "XTECH BOND 20L" - NOT MATCHED to an official product name**
- No product named "X-Tech Bond" (any spelling) exists in the ~790-entry official TDS index, and the official site search returns nothing for "Tech Bond", "XTech" or "SBR" (https://www.x-calibur.us/data_sheets_filter?query=Tech+Bond).
- Closest official product by chemistry and pack: **X-Roc Latex** - "Multi purpose bonding agent and performance enhancer for screeds and mortars". TDS: "X-Roc Latex is a styrene butadiene rubber latex used to improve the mechanical and waterproofing properties of sand cement based renders, screeds and mortars." Packaging: "20 and 200 liter drums (International)". Standards: "ASTM C1059 Type II", "DCL Green Building Regulations". Coverage: "As a bonding agent (neat): up to ... (8m2 per liter). As a bonding agent (1:1 dilution): up to ... (16m2 per liter)."
  - Listing: https://www.x-calibur.us/products/concrete-repair-and-finishes/bond-cure-and-reinforcement-protection#pro_id_158
  - TDS: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Roc_Latex1.pdf
- Other bonding products in the same official category: X-Roc MultiBond LX ("Multi-purpose polymer for water resistant renders and screeds, bonding of mortars and screeds, repair and patching of concrete"; 5, 20 and 200 kg containers; ASTM C1059 Type II; TDS does not state SBR), X-Roc MultiBond GP, X-Roc MultiBond PVA, X-Roc Acrylic Latex (acrylic, not SBR).
- Differences to flag: (a) the name itself; "X-Tech" is the flooring family and no bonding agent carries it officially; (b) price list unit of measure is "KG" with weight 20 while the name says 20L; (c) price list consumption "10sqm/litre" matches neither official figure for X-Roc Latex (8 m2/L neat, 16 m2/L diluted 1:1).
- Possibility not excluded: a locally packed or regional-only product (for example from the Nairobi operation) that is not on the international site. Not verified. Do not publish a product page under an official name until the owner photographs the pail label.

**Line 2 - "X TECH SL1 17L 7035" - MATCHED: X-Tech EpoxyFloor SL (grade SL1), RAL 7035**
- Official name: "X-Tech EpoxyFloor SL"; grade "X-Tech EpoxyFloor SL1, thickness: 1 to 1.5mm". Official one-liner: "Abrasion and chemical resistant self-leveling epoxy floor".
- Chemistry: "three component, self smoothing epoxy floor topping". VOC "<10g/L".
- Uses: "Food and beverage plants, Pharmaceutical facilities, Kitchens and laundries, Hospitals, Laboratories and clean rooms, Chemical handling and processing areas".
- Packaging and coverage: "X-Tech EpoxyFloor SL: 17 liter packs"; "X-Tech EpoxyFloor SL1: 17m2 per 17L pack at 1mm" (equals the price list's 1 L per sqm).
- Colour: "RAL 7035 Light grey" is the first of ten listed RAL colours, so "7035" on the price list is the colour code.
- Compliance printed: "SCAQMD Rule 1113", "LEED NC2009 IEQ 4.2", "FDA CFR 21 Section 175.300", "DCL Green Building Regulations"; fire: "UK Building Regulations (Document B): Class O", "BS 476 Part 7: Class 1 Surface Spread of Flame". EFNARC and FeRFA type classifications are printed for SL2, SL3 and SL4 only, not for SL1, so do not claim them for SL1.
- Primers named in the TDS for this system: X-Prime SF and X-Prime MT100 (not X-Prime GP; see line 3).
- Listing: https://www.x-calibur.us/products/flooring-systems/resin-systems#pro_id_230
- TDS: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Tech_EpoxyFloor_SL.pdf
- Differences: price list drops "EpoxyFloor" and the hyphen; official spelling is "self-leveling" (US), price list "SELF LEVELLING".

**Line 3 - "X PRIME GP 18L" - MATCHED: X-Prime GP**
- Official one-liner: "General Purpose primer for epoxy coating and floor systems". Description: "X-Prime GP is a high penetration primer suitable for use with a wide range of epoxy coatings and floor systems."
- Chemistry/type: two-part, "Easy volume ratio 1:1"; "Volume solids 90%"; "VOC = <100 g/L"; "Damp tolerant".
- Packaging: "18, 40 and 400 liter packs". Shelf life 18 months.
- Coverage printed: "5m2 per liter at 200 microns, 10m2 per liter at 100 microns".
- Compliance printed: "SCAQMD Rule 1113", "LEED NC2009 IEQ 4.2".
- Listing: https://www.x-calibur.us/products/coating-systems/primers#pro_id_845
- TDS: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Prime_GP.pdf
- Differences: (a) the price list calls it "SOLVENT BASED"; the TDS never uses that phrase and instead says "High solids" at 90% volume solids, so do not write "solvent based" as an official description; (b) "7.5 SQM/ L AT 150MC" is not printed in the TDS. The TDS gives only the 100 and 200 micron figures; simple arithmetic for a 150 micron wet film is 6.67 m2 per litre, and 7.5 is merely the midpoint of 5 and 10. Use the two official figures on the website. (c) A newer "X-Prime GP2" (100% volume solids) also exists: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Prime_GP2.pdf - confirm which one is stocked.

**Line 4 - "X SHIELD FLEXPRUF 22KGS" - MATCHED: X-Shield FlexPruf**
- Official one-liner: "High performance polymer modified elasto-cement waterproof coating". Description: "X-Shield FlexPruf is a high performance, flexible elasto-cement coating used for waterproofing of concrete and masonry."
- Composition: "two component cementitious coating manufactured using selected cements, fillers and a high performance elastic co-polymer".
- Uses: "Wet area waterproofing, Roof waterproofing, Swimming pools, Tile/stone waterproof backing, Water tank coating, Concrete protection, Waterproofing protection layer, Resurfacing, Anti root membrane".
- Packaging: "22kg kits"; "Yield 12.8 liters per kit". Colour: "Grey and white, custom colors available".
- Coverage: "1L (1.7kg) per m2 per coat ... Normal applications require 2 coats" (equals the price list's 3.4 kg/sqm in two coats).
- Compliance printed: "SCAQMD Rule 1113", "LEED NC2009 IEQ 4.2", "Classified under BS EN 1062-1:2002 as Class C1 carbon dioxide permeability", "ANSI A118.10", "BS 6920", "DCL Green Building Regulations". Advantage printed: "Can be used in contact with potable water".
- Listing: https://www.x-calibur.us/products/coating-systems/general-purpose-waterproof-coatings#pro_id_344
- TDS: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Shield_FlexPruf.pdf
- Differences: the price list says "ACRYLIC WP COAT". The word "acrylic" does not appear anywhere in the official TDS. The product is a two-component polymer-modified cementitious ("elasto-cement") coating. X-Calibur's actual acrylic coating is a different product, X-Shield AcrylCoat ("Single component elastic acrylic waterproof coating", 20 liter pails). Do not label FlexPruf "acrylic" on the site. A related "X-Shield FlexPruf Plus" (25kg kits, elongation >300%) also exists.

**Line 5 - "X SEAL PG 4L" - MATCHED BY INFERENCE: X-Seal PS800, pouring grade**
- No product named "X-Seal PG" exists officially. Three data points triangulate to X-Seal PS800 pouring grade: the price list says "PS" (polysulphide) and "POURABLE"; the TDS says "Pouring grade: 4 liter pack comprising separate base and curing agent"; and the TDS quantity table row "25 x 15 ... 0.37 ... 2.70" (metre run per litre) equals the price list's "2.7M PER L FOR 25X15 JOINT".
- Official one-liner: "High performance polysulphide joint sealant". Description: "X-Seal PS800 is a high performance polysulphide sealant for use in a variety of applications. It has excellent adhesion to most substrates and can be used in immersed conditions." "Available in gun and pouring grades".
- Uses: "High movement joints, General purpose joint sealing, Joint sealing of: Highway structures, Water retaining and excluding structures, Basement cladding, Floors, Subways".
- Colour: "Pouring grade: Grey." VOC pouring grade 42 g/L.
- Compliance printed: "SCAQMD Rule 1168", "LEED NC2009 IEQ 4.1", "Estidama LBi-2.1", "British Standard BS 4254", "British Standard BS 6920", "ASTM C920", "DCL Green Building Regulations", "ASTM D1149 (Ozone Resistance)"; "Movement Accommodation (BS6093) Butt joints: 25% Lap joints: 50%".
- Listing: https://www.x-calibur.us/products/joints/joint-sealants#pro_id_240
- TDS: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Seal_PS8001.pdf
- Differences: "PG" is shorthand for pouring grade, not a product name; official spelling of the chemistry is "polysulphide". Owner should confirm the can reads "X-Seal PS800".

**Line 6 - "X SEAL PRIMER 1L" - MATCHED BY INFERENCE: X-Seal PS800 Primer**
- The PS800 TDS lists "PS800 Primer: 1 liter can" and instructs "Use X-Seal PS800 Primer."
- Official one-liner: "High performance primer for X-Seal PS800 Sealant". Description: "X-Seal PS800 Primer is a high penetration primer for use with X-Seal PS800 joint sealant."
- Data: "1 liter packs"; "VOC <400 g/L"; "X-Seal PS800 Primer - 15m2 per liter. 1 Litre of X-Seal PS800 Primer is sufficient for 225lm of 10mm joint." Compliance printed: "DCL Green Building Regulations".
- Listing: https://www.x-calibur.us/products/joints/joint-sealants#pro_id_1075
- TDS: https://www.x-calibur.us/public/uploads/data_sheets/technical_data_sheets/X-Seal_PS800_Primer.pdf
- Differences: (a) "10% OF SEALANT VOLUME" is a rule of thumb that is not in the TDS; the official figure is area based (15 m2 per litre); (b) other X-Seal primers exist (X-Seal EP Primer, X-Seal FR200 Primer), so confirm the label; (c) the two official documents disagree on the priming window: the PS800 sealant TDS says apply sealant "between 1 and 3 hours after priming" and re-prime after 3 hours, while the primer TDS says re-prime only if left "more than 24 hours". Do not state an overcoat window on the website; link the TDS instead.
