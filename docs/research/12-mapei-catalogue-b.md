# 12 - Mapei catalogue research, part B: Flooring line (items 27-36) and Waterproofing (items 37-49)

Research date: 2026-11-05
Client: Sahihi Construction Chemicals Limited (authorized Mapei distributor, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania)
Scope: 24 price-list lines, official Mapei facts only, every fact tied to a source ID from the source list below.

## Method and limits

- What worked: official Mapei PDF documents on `cdnmedia.mapei.com` (technical data sheets, system data sheets, brochures, one Declaration of Performance) were downloaded directly and read in full as text. Where a Mapei UAE edition exists (edition code ending "(UAE)", publisher Mapei Construction Chemicals L.L.C, Dubai) it was preferred, because most of these items carry the "ME" Middle East designation. Otherwise the current Mapei S.p.A. international English edition ("en (IT)") was used.
- What did not work: every HTML product page on `www.mapei.com` (UAE, Kenya, international and other country sites) returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare bot block) to direct fetch. One retry of a single page with a browser-style user-agent header also returned 403; nothing further was tried to get around the block, and no browser automation, proxy, reader or archive service was used. Product-page URLs listed below were seen only as search-engine results; their content was read only through the search snippet, and that is stated wherever it is the only source (source S23).
- The web-search budget for this session was shared by 14 agents and was exhausted for most of the run. Because the PDF library uses hashed file names, a data sheet can only be located through search. Result: product-specific data sheets were eventually retrieved for 21 of the 23 products (24 price-list lines). The two exceptions are Mapefloor Finish 415 ME (no ME data sheet found) and Mapelastic Smart (current product TDS not found; a Mapei brochure, the Mapei Australia catalogue and a Mapei UK outline specification were used). Facts from those secondary Mapei documents are labelled as such; anything not covered is marked "not verified". For Lamposilex and Planiseal 88 only the Mapei Australia TDS edition was found; it is used with that caveat.
- Mapei East Africa (`www.mapei.com/ke/en-ke`) could not be read at all (403). Nothing in this report is confirmed against the Kenya site. Not verified: which of these products Mapei East Africa lists, and under which names.
- Official pack image URLs: none could be collected, because images are only linked from the blocked product pages. All "pack image" fields are "not verified".
- Quotations are verbatim from the source, except that en dashes inside quoted Mapei text have been replaced by plain hyphens (house rule). Typos inside Mapei's own documents are reproduced and flagged, not corrected.
- "Derived" marks simple arithmetic done by the researcher from Mapei figures; it is not a Mapei statement.
- Regional caution: ME (UAE) data sheets and international data sheets for similarly named products are different documents with different values. Do not mix them. The edition used is stated for every item.

## 27. Mapefloor FC 200 ME

- **Price-list wording:** Mapefloor FC 200 ME (Part A 22 kg L Grey + Part B 3.5 kg) | high build resin coating for floors | 0.60 kg/sqm/2 coat
- **Official name:** Mapefloor FC 200 ME (TDS title in capitals: MAPEFLOOR FC 200 ME). Price-list name matches. [S1]
- **Official description (quote):** "High build epoxy resin floor coating" (TDS subtitle). "Mapefloor FC 200 ME is a two-component epoxy formulation with high solid content used to create multi-layered resin coatings with a non-slip, or roll coat/ structured finish." [S1]
- **Family / line:** Resin flooring (Mapefloor). Listed in the Mapei UAE brochure "Products and systems for resin and cementitious floors" as "Multi-layered smooth or non-slip epoxy system with high solids content for industrial and car-park floors." [S7]
- **Classification and standards:** The UAE TDS states no EN 13813 or EN 1504-2 classification and no CE marking. Performance values are given against ASTM and EN test methods only (see data). Do not claim an EN class. [S1]
- **Where used:** [S1]
  - Chemical and pharmaceutical industries
  - Foodstuffs industry
  - Car parks
  - Aseptic rooms
  - Warehouses
  - Shopping centers
  - Factories, hangars, workshop and garages
  - Substrate: concrete floors, compressive strength at least 25 N/mm², tensile strength at least 1.5 N/mm²; moisture content not higher than 4%, no capillary rising damp
- **Key technical data:** [S1]
  - Technology: "epoxy floor coating based on solvent-free technology", "coloured dual-pack system, base, and hardener"
  - Colour: Component A "RAL colours"; Component B "clear, light yellow"
  - Mixing ratio: Component A : Component B = 22 : 3.5
  - Mix density: 1.50 g/cm³
  - Pot life: 35-45 min (TDS refers pot life to +25°C in the text; table header says +23°C and 50% R.H.)
  - Overcoating time: 20 h; set to foot traffic: 24 h; set to vehicular traffic: 48 h
  - Application temperature range: +5°C to +35°C; air relative humidity not greater than 75%
  - Final performance (7 days at +23°C): tensile strength 12 N/mm² (ASTM D 638); compressive strength >80 N/mm² (ASTM C 579) and 40 N/mm² (EN 196-1); flexural strength >40 N/mm² (ASTM D 790) and 35 N/mm² (EN 196-1); bond strength ≥ 1.5 N/mm² (ASTM D4541); Shore D 60-80; Taber test (CS17, 1000 g, 1000 cycles) 95 (unit not printed in the TDS)
  - Consumption, roll coat / structured finish: 1st coat 0.32 kg/m², finishing coat 0.32 kg/m²
  - Consumption, non-slip: 1st coat 0.35-0.40 kg/m², broadcast Quartz 0.5 at 0.5-1.0 kg/m², finishing coat 0.35-0.40 kg/m²
  - Storage: 24 months, +5°C to +35°C
  - UV note: "may change colour or fade if exposed to sunlight but this has no effect on its performance characteristics"
- **Packaging (Mapei):** "25.5 kg kits (component A = 22 kg; component B = 3.5 kg) in containers." [S1]
- **System build-up (Mapei):**
  - Roll coat / structured: Primer SN (if required, 0.15-0.25 kg/m²) -> Mapefloor FC 200 ME 1st coat -> Mapefloor FC 200 ME finishing coat. [S1]
  - Non-slip: Primer SN (if required) -> Mapefloor FC 200 ME + full broadcast Quartz 0.5 -> Mapefloor FC 200 ME finishing coat. [S1] [S7]
  - Optional: "If required Mapefloor FC 200 ME can be overcoated with Mapefloor Finish 450 ME". [S1]
  - Also listed as an optional topcoat over the Mapefloor EP19 ME trowelled epoxy screed system. [S7]
- **Product page URL:** not verified. Expected pattern https://www.mapei.com/ae/en/products-and-solutions/products/detail/mapefloor-fc-200-me returned HTTP 403 and was not confirmed by a search result.
- **TDS PDF:** S1 (URL in source list).
- **Pack image URL:** not verified.
- **Discrepancies vs price list:**
  - Consumption: price list 0.60 kg/sqm for 2 coats; Mapei gives 0.32 + 0.32 = 0.64 kg/m² (roll coat) or 0.70-0.80 kg/m² (non-slip, two coats, derived). Minor, flag.
  - "L Grey" is the stocked colour; Mapei only says "RAL colours". The RAL number of the stocked grey is not verified.
  - Description: price list "high build resin coating for floors"; Mapei "High build epoxy resin floor coating". Use Mapei wording (it is epoxy).
