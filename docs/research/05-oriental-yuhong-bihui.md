# 05 - Brand dossiers: Oriental Yuhong (OYH) and BIHUI

Research date: 2026-11-05. Prepared for the Sahihi Construction Chemicals Limited website build (sahihicc.com).

## Headline findings

1. **OYH is now the master brand.** The official global site is https://www.oyh.com/ and it brands the group as "OYH". "Oriental Yuhong" survives in the legal name, the export portal's About page and social handles. "YUHONG" is the flagship product brand inside the group. The hub page must target all three spellings.
2. **OYH publishes a full export catalogue we can build from.** https://order.oyh.com/ lists 16 categories and about 220 product pages (221 distinct product URLs in its category menu on the fetch date), each with a Download tab for the TDS. Waterproofing is the core: YHShield (SBS/APP torch-on), YHStick (self-adhesive), PMH (HDPE pre-applied), PMT (TPO), YHPUcoat / YHPUroof (polyurethane), YHCoat (cementitious and acrylic), YHCrystal (crystalline).
3. **OYH has East African references in its own words.** A 2018 Oriental Yuhong press release names "Mombasa-Nairobi Railway, Tanzania Kiggobni Sea-crossing Bridge and Angola Fevereiro International Airport" as projects it undertook. The official strategy page lists Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda and Ethiopia among countries reached. No African plant or subsidiary and no named African distributor is published.
4. **BIHUI is a tiling TOOLS brand, not a tile adhesive or grout brand.** The teal-square logo on the Sahihi business card matches the BIHUI professional tiling tools logo (visual comparison, see B1). Range: large-format tile handling, tile cutters, diamond blades and core bits, suction lifters, tile vibrators, trowels, levelling systems, mixers. No chemical products were found under this brand.
5. **Brand SERPs are empty in Tanzania.** None of the searches run returned a Tanzanian or Kenyan page for either brand. Both brand hubs grade EASY. sahihicc.com itself currently resolves to a Namecheap parking page, so there is no existing page to protect or redirect.
6. **Three content risks to settle with the owner before launch:** (a) "polyurea" - the only polyurea-class item found on OYH's export portal is one polyaspartic top coat, YHPAroof-8532; (b) sports flooring, EPDM and synthetic grass were not found in OYH's published range; (c) "authorized distributor" wording needs a document from each brand, because neither brand publishes Sahihi (or anyone) as its Tanzanian distributor.

## Method and limits

- **Sources.** Official pages were fetched directly: https://www.oyh.com/ (corporate, also served at https://www.oyhglobal.com/), https://order.oyh.com/ (export product portal; https://www.oyhorder.com/ redirects to it), nine official TDS PDFs from the portal, and one 2018 press release. For BIHUI: the Southeast Europe general representative's BIHUI site https://bihui-adria.com/ and catalogue PDF, https://kozul.hr/en/bihui-5/, two Tile and Stone Journal articles and a Stone World directory listing.
- **BIHUI's own site was not readable.** https://www.bihuitools.com/ returned HTTP 403 behind a bot challenge to every fetch. The challenge was not bypassed and no archive or proxy was used. Everything about BIHUI therefore comes from its regional representatives and trade press, plus one search-engine summary of the official About page. BIHUI's legal entity, headquarters and founding year are **not verified**.
- **Search budget ran out.** The session's shared web-search budget was exhausted early. Only 11 searches completed for this topic (5 of them BIHUI or official-site lookups). "Oriental Yuhong Kenya" ran only as a longer variant, and no product-name query (YHShield-7401, PMH-3040 and so on) could be run. Those rows are marked "not run" in the grading table.
- **SERP data is indicative only.** The search tool is US-based and returns a list of links, not a ranked google.co.tz result page. No search-volume data was available (the Ahrefs connector was not authenticated). Grades reflect "is there any local competitor page for this brand term", not measured difficulty scores.
- **Other gaps.** https://en.yuhong.com.cn/ refused the connection. The Alibaba storefront https://dongfangyuhong.en.alibaba.com/ rendered empty to the fetcher. The YHShield-7401 and YHShield-7501 TDS PDFs are scanned images with no text layer, so their figures were taken from the HTML product pages instead. Croatian product names from bihui-adria.com are translated by me.
- **No invention.** Product names are copied exactly as printed on the source page. Anything I could not confirm is marked "not verified".

## A3. Certifications and standards claimed

**Group-level list, exactly as printed under "Honors & Qualification" on https://www.oyh.com/rad.html :**
- Product Certification: "Greenguard", "Singapore GreenLabel", "Energy Star", "SIRIM", "Floor Score", "Green Mark Scheme Certification", "FM Approvals Certification", "EMICODE Certification", "CE Certification", "Environmental Product Declaration", "A+ Certification"

The page prints only the years, not the ISO numbers. These correspond in form to ISO 45001:2018, ISO 50001:2018, ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015, but the ISO numbers themselves are **not printed and not verified**.
- Laboratories: "Certified Testing Laboratories (CNAS, CMA)" - same page.
- "the group achieved EU 'CE' certification, enabling product entry into European markets" (2009) - https://www.oyh.com/about.html

**Important limit:** the page lists certification schemes, not which product holds which certificate. Never write "FM approved" or "CE marked" against a specific SKU unless that SKU's certificate is in hand.
