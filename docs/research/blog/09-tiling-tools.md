## 1. Method and limits (read first)

**What was done**

- 18 web searches (the cap for this brief), listed in section 1.1 with what came back.
- About 25 direct page fetches against the pages that ranked, to see what they contain and what they lack. **Limits that affect decisions**

1. **No search volumes.** There is no volume tool. Demand is judged only from who ranks and what kind of page it is. No volume figure appears in this file.
2. **The search tool is US-located.** Results approximate google.co.tz and google.co.ke but are not the same. Local packs, ads and local ordering were not visible.
3. **No People-Also-Ask boxes.** The tool returns links and a summary, not PAA. The questions in section 4 are marked [H] when they are a real heading or thread title on a page that ranked, and [C] when they are my candidate wording. None is a verbatim Google PAA entry.
4. **Grades are inferences.** E / M / H in section 2 are my reading of SERP composition (forums, product listings and spam pages rank = easier; trade bodies and big retailers with real guides rank = harder). They are not measured difficulty.
5. **Standards were read second-hand.** BS 5385 and ANSI A108 are paid documents. Their figures below come from the trade bodies and manufacturers that quote them (The Tile Association, NTCA's TileLetter, CTEF, Construction Specifier, a MAPEI data sheet). Each figure names the page it was read on. Confirm against the standard before a figure is published as "the standard says".
6. **Blocked pages.** toppstiles.co.uk (which now also receives the old tilewarehouse.co.uk help articles by redirect), ctm.co.ke and in.weber returned HTTP 403. Anything from those is "search summary only" and is flagged. floortrendsmag.com has been discontinued and redirects. kabetepoly.ac.ke has an expired certificate. The Rwanda TVET module page is behind a login.
7. **BIHUI's own guidance was not found.** No BIHUI chart for clips per m2, clip-to-tile-thickness fit beyond the one-line list entries, or instructions was read (bihuitools.com blocked earlier fetches, see research 05). Figures of that kind below come from other makers and are labelled as such. Ask BIHUI for theirs before publishing a number next to a BIHUI product.
8. **BIHUI status on the site.** GOVERNANCE.md holds BIHUI at "brand partner" until an appointment letter arrives, and OPEN-QUESTIONS Q10 (what is actually stocked) is open. The owner list is treated here as the range on offer, not as confirmed stock.
9. **No prices.** Several ranking pages print prices. None is repeated here.

### 3.5 Post 5 - How to Grout Tiles Without Leaving a Haze

**Outline**

1. What haze is: grout residue left to cure on the tile face. Prevention is timing and clean water.
2. Before grouting: joints clean and raked back, adhesive hardened.
3. Mixing the grout (link to post 6).
4. The float: fill diagonally, then strike off with the float on edge.
5. The wait: when the grout turns matt.
6. The sponge: damp not wet, diagonal passes, two buckets.
7. The last step: dry cloth for the powdery film.
8. Epoxy grout is different: clean at once, emulsify with a pad, cellulose sponge, change the sponge when loaded.
9. If haze is already there: what the data sheets name.
10. Textured and porous tiles: test first.
11. FAQ and enquiry.
